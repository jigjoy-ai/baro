# ADR-0005: Document connect's real flags; do not invent `--name`

**Status:** Accepted
**Context:** The goal's flag list for connect names `--name`, but a grep of crates/baro-tui/src finds no such flag: run_connect's loop (main.rs:671-720) accepts --service, --token, --workspace/--cwd, --control-url, --install-service, --uninstall-service, --once, --no-service, and ServiceConfig is `{exe, token, workspace, control_url}` (service.rs:11-20) with baked argv `connect --service --token <t> --workspace <w>` (service.rs:64-74). Help text that advertises a flag the parser silently ignores (unknown args hit `_ => i += 1` at main.rs:718) is worse than omitting it, and adding the flag is out of scope per the stated non-goals.
**Decision:** `CONNECT_USAGE` documents exactly the flags the parser accepts today: `--token <t>`, `--workspace <dir>` (note `--cwd` as its alias on the same line), `--control-url <url>`, `--install-service`, `--uninstall-service`, `--once`, `--service`, `--no-service`, `-h, --help`, `--version`. Do NOT list `--name`. Do NOT add a `--name` flag, a ServiceConfig field, or any parsing change to run_connect beyond deleting the dead help arm. `WATCH_USAGE` documents only the positional `<run-id>` (watch does no flag parsing, run_registry.rs:420-448); `STOP_USAGE` only `<run-id>`; `RUNS_USAGE` and `LOGIN_USAGE` state that they take no extra flags.
**Consequences:** Help text is verifiable against the parser. The `--name` item from the goal envelope is intentionally unimplemented and should be called out in the PR body alongside `Fixes #105`, so the issue reporter sees the deviation rather than discovering an ignored flag.

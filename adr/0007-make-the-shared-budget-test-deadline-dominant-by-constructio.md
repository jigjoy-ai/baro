# ADR-0007: Make the shared-budget test deadline-dominant by construction, with fixed delay and budget values

**Status:** Accepted
**Context:** The test at test/run-architect-outcome.test.ts:260-283 leaves only ~300ms of headroom (100+800ms scripted delay vs a 1200ms budget), so under load the obligation child can finish before the deadline. Delays are baked into the fake binary by `writePhasedFakeCodex` and the budget is the `--timeout-ms` CLI flag; both are test-side only, so no production change is needed. An obligation delay far above the budget but still bounded keeps total runtime safe even if an aborted child is merely awaited rather than killed.
**Decision:** In `packages/baro-orchestrator/test/run-architect-outcome.test.ts`, in the test `shares one wall-clock budget across Codex decision and obligation phases` only, change exactly three things:
- `writePhasedFakeCodex(dir, capture, { decision: 0, obligation: 10_000 })` (decision ≈0ms; obligation 10s, i.e. 5x the budget and still under the ~15s ceiling).
- `--timeout-ms` value `"2000"`.
- the stderr assertion regex updated to `/shared 2000ms phase budget/u` to match the new budget.
Keep every other assertion verbatim: `assert.notEqual(run.code, 0, run.stderr)`; `assert.equal(existsSync(join(dir, "outcome.json")), false)`; the `modelUsageEvents(run.stdout)` check for `measurement.status === "timed_out"` with `/architect-obligations/u` on `invocationId`. Do not change `writePhasedFakeCodex` itself beyond what the new argument requires (it already accepts `{decision, obligation}` and defaults to 0), do not touch `writeSlowFakeHarness`, `runArchitect`, or `runScript`, and do not change any other test in the file — in particular the tight-margin decision-phase test at :285-329 (`writeSlowFakeHarness(..., 500)` vs `--timeout-ms 50`) is only MENTIONED in the PR description, never edited. Do not modify `packages/baro-orchestrator/scripts/run-architect.ts`.
**Consequences:** The decision phase consumes ≈0ms of the 2000ms budget, so the obligation phase always aborts at ~2000ms while its child still has ~8s of scripted sleep left — the deadline is structurally guaranteed to fire during obligations. Worst case wall time is bounded by the 10s fake-child sleep, under the ~15s ceiling. Because the budget appears in the stderr message, the regex must be updated in lockstep with `--timeout-ms`; agents changing one without the other will see the assertion fail immediately.
